How does it work?

You choose the apps that distract you. When you open one of them, the lock appears. To unlock the app for a while, read the verse and prayer of the day and hold "Amen". You choose the length of the window in Settings: 10 or 30 minutes, 3 or 12 hours, or 1 day. When it ends, the apps are locked again.

I need to use an app right now and can't pray. What can I do?

The lock screen always has an "Emergency" button. It unlocks the apps for 15 minutes without the prayer, but it resets your current streak. Your best streak stays saved.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Knees First > Cancel Subscription (Apple help).
  • Android: open your subscriptions in Google Play, choose Knees First and tap Cancel subscription (Google help).

Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription.

What happens when my subscription ends?

All locks are removed and the app shows the subscription screen. The app never leaves apps locked for someone who is not paying.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app.

On the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchases" with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy. The chosen apps stay only on the old phone, so you will need to choose them again. Your streak does not move to the new phone either, except on iPhone if you restore an iCloud or computer backup; then your streak and settings may come back.

Refunds

Refunds are decided by the stores. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: refund policies.

How do I turn the lock off for good?

In the app, remove the apps from the lock list. You can also remove the permission (iPhone: Settings > Screen Time; Android: system settings, under Usage access and Display over other apps) or uninstall the app.

How do I stop notices or the daily reminder?

In the app, go to Settings > Privacy and notices and turn off "Get notices" or "Daily prayer reminder". You can also turn off notifications for Knees First in your phone's settings.

Privacy

The app has no account and never sends the apps you lock, your Screen Time data or your streak off your phone. Usage analytics and notices are optional and happen only if you agree. Read the Privacy Policy.
